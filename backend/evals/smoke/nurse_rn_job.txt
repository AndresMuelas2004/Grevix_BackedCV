Registered Nurse, Telemetry (Full-Time, Nights)
Quarry Hill Medical Center · Columbus, OH · On-site

About the role
Quarry Hill Medical Center is hiring a registered nurse for its 32-bed telemetry unit. You will care for adult patients with cardiac and respiratory conditions, work closely with physicians and a care team, and help newer nurses settle in.

Requirements
- Graduate of an accredited nursing program (BSN or ADN).
- Active, unencumbered state Registered Nurse (RN) license in Ohio.
- Current Basic Life Support (BLS) certification.
- At least 2 years of acute care nursing experience.
- Strong communication and charting skills.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience on a telemetry or cardiac step-down unit.
- Spanish is preferred: many of our patients and their families speak Spanish at home.
- ACLS certification.

Benefits
Tuition assistance, health insurance and a retirement plan.
